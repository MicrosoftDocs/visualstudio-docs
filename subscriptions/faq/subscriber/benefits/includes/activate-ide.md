## How do I download, find a key, and activate the Visual Studio IDE?
Your subscription level determines which version of the Visual Studio IDE you can access. For example, a Visual Studio Professional subscription doesn't grant access to Visual Studio Enterprise. If you're not sure which version you're using, check the [subscriptions page](https://my.visualstudio.com/subscriptions) in the portal to see the available subscriptions. 

### How to download and activate 
1. Sign in to the subscriptions portal.  
0. For the latest release, locate the Visual Studio IDE tile in the Tools section and select on the **Download** link at the bottom of the benefit tile.  
0. You're redirected to the Download Results page for Visual Studio, where you have the opportunity to download the IDE.  
0. Follow the download and set up instructions. 
1. After installation, sign in to the IDE by using the email your subscription is assigned to. Visual Studio 2026 doesn't require a product key. Instead, signing in verifies the subscription and unlocks access to the IDE; **no product key is required**.

If your organization uses Volume Licensing for qualifying broad, offline, or managed deployment scenarios, Visual Studio 2026 keys may still be available through your Volume Licensing admin.

### Find an older version 
You can also search and download previous versions from the [downloads page](https://my.visualstudio.com/Downloads?q=visual%20studio&pgroup=) in the subscriptions portal.
