## How do I remove the default overallocation cap?

Default overallocation restrictions protect agreements from assigning far more subscriptions than you purchased. If you have a legitimate business case for overallocating more subscriptions, contact support by using one of the links in the **Still need help** section. Request removal of the cap and provide a business justification for doing so.
